How it works

  1. 1The X-ray is converted to grayscale, resized to 224×224 and contrast-enhanced (CLAHE), exactly as in training.
  2. 2A ResNet50 network fine-tuned on public chest X-ray datasets estimates the probability of each condition.
  3. 3Grad-CAM highlights the regions that most influenced the prediction.
